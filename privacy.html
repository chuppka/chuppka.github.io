Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The site does not use its own advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about the hosting provider's handling of website visit data.

Information stored by the game

Postag stores game progress, best distance, coin balance, cosmetic unlocks, tutorial completion, music and sound-effect volume preferences, and advertising limits locally on your device. These values may be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup settings. A user account is optional.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud saves

In versions where this feature is available, you may choose to sign in with Google to back up and restore progress. Declining sign-in leaves progress stored locally. Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication, which processes your Google account identity and basic profile information, such as your name and email address. Postag uses the Firebase account identifier to associate a cloud save with your account, and may show your display name in Settings. The Player ID shown in Settings → Privacy is that Firebase identifier and can be copied for support requests.

Cloud Firestore stores the optional cloud save. It can contain your coin balance, best distance, tutorial progress, unlocked and selected cosmetics, pending case reward, automatic-ad removal state, reward cooldown, and hashed markers for fulfilled purchases. Your birth year, advertising treatment and consent choices, and audio preferences are not included in this game-progress backup. The configured gameplay database is located in Belgium; Google may process authentication and service data in other countries. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

The account and cloud save are kept to provide optional backup until you request deletion. A cloud backup may not contain changes made after the last successful synchronization. To request deletion, use the prominent Delete Postag account link on this page.

Advertising

Postag uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising privacy choices. Depending on applicable settings, consent and the user's age category, these services may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and approximate location derived from network information for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Ad technology providers may process data outside your country.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Where enabled, purchases are processed through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google handles payment details; the game does not collect your payment card information. The game checks purchase receipts and stores product unlocks, coin grants, the automatic-ad removal entitlement, and hashed purchase-token markers locally to deliver items and prevent duplicate grants. These progress values may also be included in an optional cloud save as described above. Store-owned cosmetic purchases can be restored through Google Play. Google Play does not restore already consumed coin purchases as owned inventory; an optional progress backup is separate from the store's purchase-restoration service. Purchase availability depends on the released version.

Unity IAP may process operational purchase, installation, device, session and country information to provide its service and prevent fraud. Unity advertising and analytics consent are set to denied for this integration, and the optional Unity Analytics adapter is disabled; this does not prevent all essential SDK data processing. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity IAP privacy information. For purchase issues, contact the developer using the address below. Purchase availability depends on the released version.

Children and age-appropriate advertising

Postag is intended for a mixed audience of children and adults. The game asks for a birth year to select an advertising treatment. The entered year is used on the device and is not saved by the game or transmitted by the game as a birth year. The game retains only an advertising treatment category. If no year is supplied, child-directed treatment is used. The game requests child-directed treatment for younger or unknown-age users and under-age-of-consent treatment where applicable, and limits requested ad content to a general-audience rating. The game does not request personalized advertising for users receiving those restricted treatments.

Your choices

Where required, a consent form lets you manage advertising privacy choices before ads are requested. When applicable, Settings → Privacy in the game provides access to advertising choices so they can be reviewed or changed. Voluntary rewarded ads are optional. A qualifying in-app purchase removes automatic interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available. Purchase availability depends on the released version.

Contact, account deletion and rights

You may contact Nik at chuppka.work@gmail.com about this policy or applicable privacy rights, including questions from parents or guardians. You can request deletion of your Postag account and its associated cloud data through our account deletion page. It provides an email request route that works without reinstalling the game and is also reachable through Settings → Privacy → Privacy Policy.

After account ownership is verified, deletion removes your Postag Firebase Authentication profile and cloud game save, including the progress stored with it. Your Google account remains unaffected. We use the request and contact information to verify ownership, carry out deletion and reply. If any information must be retained for a legal or security reason, we explain the reason and retention period in our reply. Local game data can be cleared using your device's app settings; we cannot remotely erase it. Requests concerning information independently held by Google, Unity or the website host may also be directed to those providers through their privacy tools.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The current version and its update date will be available on this page.
