Postag Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Postag is developed by Nik, based in Israel. For privacy questions, contact chuppka.work@gmail.com.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The site does not use its own advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about the hosting provider's handling of website visit data.

Information stored by the game

Postag stores game progress, best distance, coin balance, cosmetic unlocks, tutorial completion, music and sound-effect volume preferences, and advertising limits locally on your device. These values may be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup settings. The game does not require a user account and does not operate its own gameplay-data server.

Advertising

Postag uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising privacy choices. Depending on applicable settings, consent and the user's age category, these services may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and approximate location derived from network information for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Ad technology providers may process data outside your country.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Where enabled, purchases are processed through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google handles payment details; the game does not collect your payment card information. The game checks purchase receipts and stores product unlocks, coin grants, the automatic-ad removal entitlement, and hashed purchase-token markers locally to deliver items and prevent duplicate grants. Store-owned cosmetic purchases can be restored through Google Play. Already consumed coin purchases and the resulting local balance cannot currently be restored reliably after reinstall or synchronized across devices.

Unity IAP may process operational purchase, installation, device, session and country information to provide its service and prevent fraud. Unity advertising and analytics consent are set to denied for this integration, and the optional Unity Analytics adapter is disabled; this does not prevent all essential SDK data processing. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity IAP privacy information. For purchase issues, contact the developer using the address below. Purchase availability depends on the released version.

Children and age-appropriate advertising

Postag is intended for a mixed audience of children and adults. The game asks for a birth year to select an advertising treatment. The entered year is used on the device and is not saved by the game or transmitted by the game as a birth year. The game retains only an advertising treatment category. If no year is supplied, child-directed treatment is used. The game requests child-directed treatment for younger or unknown-age users and under-age-of-consent treatment where applicable, and limits requested ad content to a general-audience rating. The game does not request personalized advertising for users receiving those restricted treatments.

Your choices

Where required, a consent form lets you manage advertising privacy choices before ads are requested. When applicable, Settings → Privacy in the game provides access to advertising choices so they can be reviewed or changed. Voluntary rewarded ads are optional. A qualifying in-app purchase removes automatic interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available. Purchase availability depends on the released version.

Contact and rights

You may contact Nik at chuppka.work@gmail.com about this policy or applicable privacy rights, including questions from parents or guardians. Because the game does not maintain user accounts or its own gameplay-data server, it may not be able to identify your device's local records. Local game data can be cleared using your device's app settings. Requests concerning information held by Google may also be directed to Google through its privacy tools.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The current version and its update date will be available on this page.
