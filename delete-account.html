Delete your Postag account

You can ask Nik, the developer of Postag, to delete your Postag account and its associated cloud data. You do not need to reinstall or open the game to make a request.

Send a deletion request

Email chuppka.work@gmail.com from the Google email address you use to sign in to Postag. Use the subject Postag account deletion request.

If available, include your Player ID from Settings → Privacy. It helps us find your save, but is optional; your sign-in email can be used instead.

Delete Postag account

This button opens an email draft. Review and send it to submit your request. Clicking the button alone does not submit a request or delete any data. If no email app opens, send the email yourself using the address above.

What happens next?

Requests are processed manually. We verify that you own the account before deleting anything. We will reply with the next steps and expected completion time, and confirm by email when deletion is complete. If you cannot use your sign-in email, contact the same support address so we can discuss another way to verify ownership.

Never send your Google password, verification codes or payment card details. A Player ID alone is not proof of account ownership.

What is deleted?

  • Your Postag sign-in profile stored in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your Postag cloud save in Cloud Firestore, including its coin balance, best distance, cosmetic unlocks and selections, tutorial progress, and other game-progress data stored with it.

Deleting your Postag account is permanent. Your existing Google account is not deleted. Payment records held independently by Google Play are managed by Google; account deletion does not itself request a purchase refund.

What about data on my phone?

We cannot remotely erase the game's local storage. To remove local game progress and settings, use your phone's app settings to clear Postag's storage. This removes the local progress on that device. If you have never connected Google, there is no Postag cloud account to delete.

Other information

We use your request and contact information to verify ownership, process deletion and reply to you. If any information must be retained for a legal or security reason, we explain the reason and retention period in our reply. Information processed independently by Google, Unity or the website host is described in our Privacy policy.
